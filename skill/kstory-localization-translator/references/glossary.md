# KStory translation glossary

- KStory: proper name; never translate.
- NPC: keep as `NPC` unless the target language's style guide explicitly requires another form.
- Blacksmith: `herrero` in Spanish.
- Hammer: `martillo` in Spanish.
- Shovel: `pala` in Spanish.
- Well: `pozo` in Spanish.
- Shrine: `santuario` in Spanish.
- Workbench: `banco de trabajo` in Spanish.
- Quest: `misión` in player-facing Spanish text.
- Keep item and entity names consistent when they appear as `{item}` or `{entity}` variables.
